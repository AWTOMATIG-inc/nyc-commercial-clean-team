**Oct 10, 2026** · *@Belnys*

## Summary
The developer's first round of fixes is live, but the site still has problems that hold back rankings and leads. The biggest is placeholder "lorem ipsum" text on about 74 service and area pages. Most of what's left is one-time template work for the developer, plus a few copy fixes for marketing.

This is based on a live read of `nyccleantinc.com` on Oct 9: all 100 pages in the sitemap, 25 read in full.
